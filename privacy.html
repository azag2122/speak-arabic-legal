Privacy Policy

Yalla Expat · bundle id com.zagge.speakarabic · 26 September 2026

Yalla Expat is a practice app for spoken Emirati and Gulf Arabic, made for everyday life in the UAE. It is not a news reader and it does not teach Modern Standard Arabic as a separate course. This policy covers the iOS app and the same pages when you open them in a browser.

What stays on this device

The app has no Yalla Expat account and no Yalla Expat server. Progress is stored in the browser storage on this device:

  • One progress record: stars, which tracks you have cleared, and the daily review schedule
  • Which practice recording you prefer to hear, Hamdan or Fatima
  • The daily review schedule (which phrases are due, and which are fading)
  • Whether a subscription or a debug unlock is active on this device
  • A short on-device log of product events (see Analytics)

Deleting the app deletes that storage. We cannot see it from another phone.

Microphone and speech

When you tap to speak, the app uses the microphone and speech recognition so it can tell whether the phrase was close enough. On iPhone and iPad that recognition is the system speech service. This build does not upload your recordings to a Yalla Expat server. Recordings you play back with “Play me” stay in memory for that practice turn.

iOS asks permission the first time. You can refuse. “I said it” still lets you continue without the microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple and charged to your Apple ID. Apple processes payment, renewal, and cancellation. Yalla Expat stores only the entitlement needed to unlock tracks F–P, the final exam, and Today in the UAE: whether Premium is active, which product, and when it expires. We do not receive your card number.

Analytics

This build does not send analytics to a third-party service. A small set of events (onboarding finished, a track cleared, the paywall, purchase start, success, failure, and restore) is printed to the developer console and kept in on-device storage, capped at the latest 200 events. The log has no account name and no recording. A future version may forward those same events to a provider. That will be described here before it is turned on.

Audio

Phrase audio is bundled in the app so practice works offline. Playing it does not contact a Yalla Expat server.

Children

There is one progress record on the device. The app does not ask for your name or your gender, and this version has no ads. It does not ask for a date of birth, a school, or a contact list. Hamdan and Fatima are playback voices, not profiles.

UAE

The app is meant for use in the United Arab Emirates. This draft has not been reviewed against the UAE Personal Data Protection Law or any other statute. App Studio owns that review and the final policy. Do not treat this page as a claim that the app is certified or registered.

Changes

If the app starts sending data off the device, or the subscription or speech flow changes, this page will be updated in the app. The date at the top is the date of this draft.

Contact

Until a support address is published on the App Store listing, privacy questions go to the developer of Yalla Expat, bundle id com.zagge.speakarabic, through the listing once it exists. This page is included in the app so you can read it with no network connection.
