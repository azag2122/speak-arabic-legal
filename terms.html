What the app is

Yalla Expat is practice for spoken Emirati and Gulf Arabic so you can navigate everyday life in the UAE: greetings, a café, a taxi, a shop, home, school pickup, the office, the landlord, a supermarket, a restaurant, and similar scenes. It is listening and speaking. It is not a Modern Standard Arabic course, not an alphabet course, and not a mix of dialects.

It is a learning aid. It is not a translator, a legal or medical service, or an emergency line. Check important conversations yourself.

The free path and Premium

  • Tracks A–E (greetings through food and the café) stay free.
  • Tracks F–P, the final exam, and Today in the UAE (daily review) need an active Premium subscription.
  • A track unlocks when the previous one is cleared: listen, understand, say, then the dialogue.
  • The final exam waits while too many phrases are fading. Today in the UAE is how you clear them.

Subscriptions

Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple under the group Speak Arabic Premium.

  • speakarabic.premium.monthly — monthly, planned price 7.99 USD

The paywall shows the price from the App Store when the product has loaded. Apple charges your Apple ID. The plan renews until you cancel. Cancel at least a day before renewal in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Restore purchases puts an active subscription back on this device for the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Payment, billing errors, and family sharing follow Apple’s terms. This draft does not change those.
